Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026. The German version is authoritative.

In short: Your entries stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch. No ads, no tracking, no analytics services, no server of our own. Only if you use the optional “Friends” feature are a nickname and your weekly numbers shared with your friends via Apple's iCloud.

2. Data that stays on your device

Everything you log in BierTracker (drinks with time, amount and alcohol content, your goals, your reason, evening plans, weekly reviews, settings) is stored only on your iPhone. So that widgets and the Apple Watch app show the same data, it lives in a shared app container on the device and is transferred directly between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch. We as developers have no access to it.

The estimated BAC is calculated on the device. It is an estimate, not a measurement.

3. Apple Health

If you allow it, BierTracker reads your weight, height, date of birth and sex (for the BAC estimate), your heart rate variability and resting heart rate (for the recovery insights) and alcoholic beverages already recorded. BierTracker writes your drinks as “Alcoholic Beverages” together with their calories to Apple Health.

This health data never leaves your device through BierTracker, is not shared with us or third parties and is not used for advertising. You can change access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → BierTracker.

4. Optional “Friends” feature

Only if you tap “Join in” in the Friends tab, the following is stored in the app's public iCloud database (CloudKit):

  • your chosen nickname and a random 6-character code,
  • your weekly numbers for the last 16 weeks: drinks per week, alcohol-free days, tracked days and your weekly goal,
  • only if you turn on “BAC and last drink”: drinks today, estimated BAC, the time you'll be back at zero and the time of your last drink,
  • your connections to friends, bets you create or join (type, period, stake as text) and reports you send.

Times of individual drinks, drink types and health data from Apple Health are never shared. The data is visible to people who know your code or the code of a shared bet. It is stored via your iCloud account; Apple processes it as a service provider, possibly on servers outside the EU (see Apple's privacy policy). We don't see your name or Apple ID. We see reports about other users in order to review abuse.

Deletion: Friends → profile → “Leave Friends” deletes your profile, your connections and the bets you created from iCloud.

5. What we don't do

  • no ads and no tracking across apps or websites,
  • no third-party analytics or crash reporting,
  • no selling or sharing of data.

6. Legal basis

Information about your alcohol consumption is health data. We process the Friends feature based on your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by joining and can withdraw at any time by leaving.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. Write to the email address above. You can also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority: www.dsb.gv.at.

8. Age

BierTracker is intended for adults aged 18 and over.
